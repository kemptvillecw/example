About Us

A welcoming, member-led community for writers at every stage.

Our Story

The Kemptville Creative Writers (KCW) is a branch of the North Grenville Writers Circle, founded in 2018 by Pamela Welling. We are a diverse community of writers who meet in person to learn from guest authors, explore craft topics with subject matter experts, and dedicate time to developing our writing skills.

Our members include hobbyists, aspiring novelists, memoirists, and both traditionally and independently published authors.

KCW is a member-led organization that meets every Monday at the North Grenville Public Library (NGPL), in the Tallman Room, from 6:00 p.m. to 7:50 p.m. Meetings typically include group discussions, writing exercises, or guest presentations.

We aim to host published authors or subject matter experts twice a month, based on what members want to learn or works in progress.

Membership is free, and new members are always welcome. Writers of all genres and experience levels are encouraged to join—simply attend any meeting to get started.
