About KCW

A place to learn, write, and grow together.

Our story

Creative writing, in community

The Kemptville Creative Writers (KCW) is a branch of the North Grenville Writers Circle, founded in 2018 by Pamela Welling.

We are a diverse community of writers who meet in person to learn from guest authors, explore craft topics with subject matter experts, and dedicate time to developing our writing skills.

Our members include hobbyists, aspiring novelists, memoirists, and both traditionally and self-published authors.

KCW is member-led, and our programming is shaped by what members want to learn and the projects they are working on.
