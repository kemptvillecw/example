Schedule & Events

Make time for your words.

Join us Monday evenings for writing, conversation, craft, and guest presentations.

Every Monday

Our regular meeting

KCW meetings are held at the North Grenville Public Library in the Tallman Room, Monday nights from January to May and September to December.

6:00–6:15

Opening

Welcome, announcements, and getting settled for the evening.

6:15–7:15

Guest speaker or writing prompt

Our main session may feature a published author, subject-matter expert, craft topic, or guided writing exercise.

7:15–7:45

Writing, sharing & discussion

Time to write, share work, exchange ideas, and talk about the craft.

7:45–7:50

Closing

A quick wrap-up before we head home.

What to expect

Guest presentations & member-led sessions

We aim to host published authors or subject-matter experts twice a month, shaped by member interests and works in progress.
