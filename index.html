About KCW

Kemptville Creative Writers (KCW) began as the North Grenville Writers Circle, founded in 2018 by Pamela Welling.

Today, KCW brings together writers of all levels, from those just beginning to write to experienced and published authors. Our members write across genres and forms, including fiction, non-fiction, poetry and memoir.

We meet to write, share ideas, explore the craft of writing, and learn from authors and subject-matter experts. It's a place to develop your writing, connect with other writers, and encourage one another along the way.
